The HCS Sub Hub, step by step

What an outside substitute sees on an ordinary day, in the days before they sub, and on the day itself. Then the teacher's side, the office's side, and one table of what a sub can and can't see.

Step 1

A sub on an ordinary day

Subs sign in with their @harfordchristian.org school Google account at app.harfordchristian.org or in the HCS Hubs phone app.

Nothing to do, nothing to see

  • With no assignment, the Sub Hub has one tab, My assignments, and it says so.
  • No rosters, no attendance, no Who's Out, no student lists.
  • As soon as the office's leave log names them (it's checked every 10 minutes, 6 AM–10 PM), the assignment appears here and an email goes out.

Subs who are also HCS parents

  • If FACTS lists the sub's school email as a guardian email, one sign-in covers both. A switch appears in the header: Sub · Staff · Parent.
  • On days with an assignment, the app opens on the Sub Hub. On other days it opens on the Parent Hub with their own children. Once they pick, the app remembers their choice.
  • If FACTS has only a personal address for them, they need two separate sign-ins. The fix: the office adds the school address as the guardian's second email in FACTS.

The Staff Hub, for a sub

"Staff" in the switch is a cut-down Staff Hub that subs have every day, subbing or not:

  • My Day, Search, Calendar, Tools. No Attendance tab.
  • Tools: Rainy Day Transportation, Get Help, Useful links, Talon Hub. No reports (Missing Work, Honor Roll and the rest are refused).
  • Search (student records) works only while the sub is on duty. On other days it says when it opens. See the table.
  • On My Day, the duty reminders list the covered teacher's duties on the covered days, marked like "Recess (Paige Fenwick)".
Step 2 · stage 1

The days before a sub day

From the moment the sub is assigned until their last covered day, My assignments shows a card for each cover.

What's on the card

  • Who and when: the teacher, the dates and the hours. "Next: Thu, Nov 26 · 7:45 AM to 3:45 PM".
  • Plans from the teacher: a general note and links, then a note for each day and a note under each class. They appear the moment the teacher saves them. Until then: "The teacher hasn't added plans yet".
  • Files from the teacher: links to the teacher's Drive folders (the Always folder and this absence's folder). A folder only shows once it has a file in it.
  • The day's schedule: each covered class with its time, period and room. Only the classes inside the sub's hours.
  • No student names yet. Rosters open on the day.

Coming later: a bell-schedule page, emergency procedures, the campus map and the office phone are on the roll-out plan for this card but aren't built yet. The class times on the card come from FACTS. Duties show in the Staff Hub's My Day, as above.

The emails (from noreply@)

These are the real email templates, filled in with the demo names. The sub's email links to the Sub Hub; the teacher's links to the Staff Hub. No email ever names a student or contains the sub report's text.

Step 3 · stage 2

On the sub day

From 6:30 AM on each covered day until 30 minutes after the sub's time ends, the Sub Hub opens the teacher's classes, and only the classes the sub covers. On a day the school is closed there's no cover.

Covering banner and Now / Next

  • The banner says whose classes and until when, with shortcuts.
  • Now / Next shows the class in session and the next one, with who's out of each. It updates every minute.
  • Three more tabs appear at the bottom: Attendance, Who's Out, Sub report.
  • These are the same screens teachers use. The server answers "as the covered teacher, for the covered classes", and checks the time window on every tap.

Homeroom attendance: take it and submit

Only when the sub's hours include homeroom.

  1. Everyone starts unmarked. Tap a student to cycle Present → Absent → Tardy.
  2. Mark everyone else Present fills in the rest.
  3. Submit goes to FACTS through the school's one attendance writer, the same path teachers use. It works until the end of homeroom.
  4. A parent's report shows beside the name ("Parent: absent"). Parent and office entries in FACTS are never overwritten.

The record notes that the sub submitted it on the teacher's behalf.

The sub report

  • One note for the day, plus an optional note for each class.
  • It shows whether homeroom attendance went in.
  • The sub can change it until midnight that day, even after access has ended.
  • The teacher reads it in the Staff Hub and gets an email saying a report is waiting, once the day's cover is over. The email doesn't include the report's text.

Access ends on its own

  • When the window closes, the Attendance and Who's Out tabs close and the page clears every roster and mark it was showing.
  • The card says "Finished". The plans and the Drive links are gone from it, and the Drive sharing is removed within 10 minutes.
  • Only Sub report stays, until midnight.

The office can change the window in Tools → Sub assignments: Start now (open it early on a covered day), End now (close it early), Extend (to a later time), and Undo. After End now the server refuses the sub within about a minute; their screen closes the next time it checks (their next tap, when they come back to the app, or within 10 minutes).

Step 4

The teacher's side

Teachers do this in the Staff Hub. It only applies to outside subs on the leave log. HCS staff covering in-house don't get a Sub Hub, plans or reports.

"Your sub" on My Day

  • Who is coming and when, and whether plans are written ("No plans yet" or "Your plans are ready ✓").
  • Add plans opens Tools → My sub plans.
  • When a sub leaves a report, a Read button appears.

Writing plans

  1. Notes for the sub: where things are, routines, who to ask.
  2. Links (up to 6, https only), such as a lesson folder or slides. The teacher shares those files themselves.
  3. A note for each day and a box for each class the sub takes that day.
  4. Save plans. The sub sees them at once.

Past days keep what was written. A cancelled cover can't be edited.

Adding files in Google Drive

Optional, and off until each teacher turns it on. Everything lives on the school's shared drive "HCS Sub Plans".

Subs are never given the teacher's top folder, so they never see an earlier absence's files.

  1. In Tools → My sub plans, tick Use a Drive folder for my subs. The Hub makes the teacher's folder and its Always folder on HCS Sub Plans, and makes the teacher Content manager of their own folder.
  2. Tap the Always folder link and drag in what every sub needs: syllabus, seating chart, routines.
  3. On an absence's card, tap Make a folder for this absence, then Open this absence's folder, and drop in that day's handouts. Only that sub sees it. Or tick No folder this time.
  4. That's all. Within 10 minutes the sub gets view access, and the folder appears under "Files from …" on their card. Google sends the sub no email about it.

An empty folder never shows. The sub gets a link only to a folder with at least one file in it. Access lasts only while the cover is coming up or under way. It's removed when the cover ends or is cancelled, when a different sub is named, or when the teacher turns Drive off. Turning it off keeps the folder and files.

Reading the sub report afterwards

  • At the bottom of My sub plans, under Sub reports: the last 30 days, newest first.
  • Each one shows whether homeroom attendance was submitted, the sub's note, and any class notes.
  • The teacher also gets a "Sub report from …" email with a link here.
Step 5

The office's side

Staff Hub → Tools. The sub coordinators and administrators have both tools.

  • Every sub named in the leave log, marked External (uses the Sub Hub) or In-house (HCS staff covering; listed for the full picture only).
  • For each external sub: "Teacher's plans written ✓" with the time, or "No plans from the teacher yet". Show plans opens them read-only. Only the teacher can write them.
  • Buttons: Start now (on a covered day), End now, Extend…, Undo, and Cancel for assignments the office added.
  • Add an assignment for one the leave log doesn't have. A leave-log name that doesn't match anyone in FACTS appears at the top with a form to link it to a school account.
  • Every sub today: covering now, later today, or finished, with their hours and that day's classes and rooms.
  • Today's part of the teacher's plans sits under each sub, so the front desk can print it and hand it over.
  • No email addresses on this page.
